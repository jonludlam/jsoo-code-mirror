# Cm_theme_one_dark

`__CM__theme_one_dark` exposes `oneDark`, `oneDarkTheme`,
`oneDarkHighlightStyle`, `color`. Bind `one_dark : Extension.t`,
`one_dark_theme : Extension.t`, `one_dark_highlight_style :
Cm_language.HighlightStyle.t`, and `color` as a record of the palette
strings. Depends on Cm_state, Cm_view, Cm_language.
